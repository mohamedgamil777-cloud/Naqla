**Creative Direction**

**ENQELLY \| انقلي**

# 1. Brand Big Idea

The creative platform should own one simple, memorable Egyptian
conversation:

**مين جاي؟  
انقلي جاي.**

When people need something moved, the question becomes “Who is coming?”
The answer is Enqelly. This idea should be flexible enough to power
Reels, social posts, advertising and brand communication.

# 2. Brand Personality

- Smart, simple and easy to understand.

- Egyptian and conversational, without being overly casual.

- Professional and trustworthy, without looking corporate or cold.

- Modern, confident and action-oriented.

- Helpful and accessible for everyday customers.

# 3. Visual World

Recommended direction: Modern Egyptian Editorial Design.

The brand should use one coherent visual language rather than mixing
unrelated illustration styles. The visual world combines clean geometric
forms, premium simplified environments, strong typography and purposeful
motion.

## Core Principles

- Minimal compositions with a clear focal point.

- Real Egyptian life interpreted through a polished, modern visual lens.

- Bold typography as a primary design element.

- Movement and direction built into the composition.

- One clear message per frame.

# 5. Typography

Typography should feel bold, modern and highly legible on a mobile
screen.

- Arabic: use a bold modern Arabic sans-serif with clear letterforms.

- English: use a clean geometric sans-serif that complements the Arabic
  wordmark.

- Keep on-screen copy short and impactful.

- Prioritize hierarchy: one message, one focal point, one action.

Example sequence: بتَنقل؟ → مين جاي؟ → انقلي جاي.

# 6. Motion Language

Motion should feel like transportation: directional, smooth, purposeful
and controlled.

- Sliding objects and directional transitions.

- Route lines that guide the eye.

- Smooth zooms and controlled fast cuts.

- Package and location elements that transform between scenes.

Avoid random spinning, excessive bouncing, generic transitions and
multiple animation styles within the same video.

## Signature Motion Asset

An orange route line travels across the screen and transforms into the
next scene. This can become a recognizable Enqelly signature across
future Reels.

# 7. Content Worlds

## World A — Brand Motion

Premium illustration and motion graphics for launch campaigns,
explainers, service announcements and brand storytelling.

## World B — Real Life

High-quality photography and video featuring real vehicles, drivers,
deliveries and behind-the-scenes content.

Rule: Do not randomly mix generic AI photography and unrelated cartoon
styles in one creative.

# 8. The Enqelly Social Formula

**Hook:** Capture attention with a bold question or relatable situation.

**Problem:** Show a real need: boxes, packages, furniture or goods that
need to move.

**Answer:** Introduce Enqelly naturally as the solution.

**Value:** Communicate the service benefits simply.

**Brand Moment:** End with a memorable message and a clean logo reveal.

# Creative North Star

Enqelly should feel like a modern Egyptian technology and logistics
brand—not simply a traditional moving company.

**Bold Egyptian language + Premium minimal design + Movement as a visual
signature**
