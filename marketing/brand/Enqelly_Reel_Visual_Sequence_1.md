**Reel Visual Sequence**

**ENQELLY \| انقلي**

A premium redesign concept for the Enqelly launch Reel. The objective is
to retain the strong “مين جاي؟” concept while creating a more unified
visual and motion experience.

# Reel Concept

**بتنقل؟ → مين جاي؟ → انقلي جاي.**

# Scene 1 — Hook \| 0–3 sec

Visual: Warm cream background. Large black Arabic typography appears
with generous spacing.

Copy: بتَنقل؟

Motion: An orange route line enters quickly from the side and creates
forward momentum.

Goal: Stop the scroll immediately.

# Scene 2 — The Need \| 3–8 sec

Visual: A clean, modern illustrated Egyptian home environment. Boxes and
goods move toward the door.

Copy: وعندك حاجات محتاجة تتنقل؟

Motion: Objects slide naturally toward a common direction.

Goal: Create an instantly relatable situation.

# Scene 3 — The Question \| 8–11 sec

Visual: The movement pauses. The composition becomes minimal.

Copy: مين جاي؟

Motion: A brief pause creates anticipation before the answer.

Goal: Build the campaign's central question.

# Scene 4 — Brand Reveal \| 11–16 sec

Visual: The orange route line races across the screen and transforms
into the Enqelly visual world.

Copy: انقلي جاي.

Motion: Fast, controlled transition followed by a confident logo or
symbol reveal.

Goal: Establish the brand as the answer.

# Scene 5 — Service Montage \| 16–26 sec

Visual: Three fast, consistent scenes showing goods, packages and moving
needs. Use the same illustration style throughout.

Suggested copy: كراتين وحاجات / نقل وشحن / من مكان لمكان

Motion: Directional transitions connected by the signature orange route
line.

Goal: Explain what Enqelly does without overloading the viewer.

# Scene 6 — Benefits \| 26–30 sec

Visual: Clean icon-led or typographic sequence.

Copy: العربية المناسبة / سواق محترف / حجز سهل

Motion: Each benefit enters with a simple directional movement.

Goal: Build confidence and simplify the value proposition.

# Scene 7 — Final Brand Moment \| 30–34 sec

Visual: Deep teal background. Minimal, premium composition.

Copy: مين جاي؟

انقلي جاي.

Motion: Clean logo reveal followed by the orange route line settling
beneath the message.

Goal: Leave one memorable brand message.

# Production Rules

- Format: 9:16 vertical, optimized for Reels.

- Use one unified illustration and motion style from beginning to end.

- Keep text large enough to read instantly on a phone.

- Limit each scene to one core message.

- Use Deep Teal, Orange, Warm Cream and Near Black consistently.

- Use the orange route line as the recurring motion signature.

- Keep the full logo treatment primarily for the final brand moment.

# Creative Standard

The final Reel should feel like a professionally art-directed brand
film: simple enough to understand in seconds, but distinctive enough to
establish a repeatable Enqelly visual identity.
